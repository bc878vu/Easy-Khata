Privacy Policy — Easy Khata

Food Money Ledger · 9 November 2026

Seedhi si baat: aap ka hisaab, aap ka data hai. Hum isay na bechte hain, na ads ke liye use karte hain, aur na kisi ko dete hain — sirf wahi dikhta hai jo aap khud share karna chahein.

1. Hum kya save karte hain

  • Account: aap ka naam, email, aur phone number (agar aap dein), aur profile photo (agar aap lagayein).
  • Hisaab: aap ke dost (contacts), transactions (kitne diye, kitne liye), settlements, aur community memberships.
  • Notifications: aap ki notification settings aur push ka token — taake transaction ki khabar aap tak pahunche.
  • Bina login ke (Guest mode): aap ka hisaab sirf aap ke apne phone/browser par save hota hai — humare servers par kuch nahi jata.

2. Ye data kis liye hai

  • Hisaab dikhane ke liye — kis ne kitne diye, kis se kitne lene hain, balance kya hai.
  • Aap ke hisaab ko aap ke doosre devices par bhi dikhane ke liye (jab aap account banayein).
  • Transaction ki yaad-dahani aur notifications bhejne ke liye.
  • Community ka hisaab sirf us community ke members dekh sakte hain — baaki koi nahi.

3. Hum kya NAHI karte

  • Aap ka data kabhi nahi bechte.
  • App mein koi ads nahi hain, aur aap ka data ads ke liye use nahi hota.
  • Aap ka data kisi teesri party ko nahi dete — sirf neeche likhe service providers data ko service chalane ke liye use karte hain.

4. Aap ka data kahan rehta hai

Account ka data mehfooz tareeqe se Supabase (database aur login) aur Firebase (push notifications) par save hota hai. App Vercel par hosted hai. Ye providers data ko sirf service dene ke liye use karte hain, apni privacy policies ke mutabiq.

5. Aap ke rights

  • Profile details (naam, phone, photo) aap khud kabhi bhi badal sakte hain.
  • Record delete karne par wo sirf aap ki history se hat-ta hai — doosre dost ke hisaab par koi fark nahi parta. Settled hisaab hamesha locked rehta hai, taake purana hisaab dobara na badla ja sake.
  • Account band karwana ho to WhatsApp helpline par rabta karein — confirm karne ke baad aap ka account data delete kar diya jayega.
  • Notifications aap Account menu se ya phone ki settings se kabhi bhi band kar sakte hain.

6. Bachche

Ye app aam users ke liye hai — 13 saal se kam umar ke bachon ke liye nahi. Hum jaan-boojh kar 13 saal se kam umar ke bachon ka data collect nahi karte.

7. Is policy mein tabdeeli

Agar policy mein kuch badla, to nayi version isi page par lag jayegi aur upar ki date bhi badal jayegi.
