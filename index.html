Privacy Policy — Easy Khata

Aap ka hisaab, aap ka data. Aap ke records sirf aap ke apne account mein save hote hain (Supabase cloud) — koi aur user aap ke records nahi dekh sakta. Bina login ke (guest mode) hisaab sirf is phone/browser par save hota hai, kahin upload nahi hota.

Kya save hota hai: aap ka naam, phone, doston ke naam aur un ke saath len-den (amount, date, note), profile photo aur notification token (sirf aap ko popup bhejne ke liye).

Kya share nahi hota: aap ka data kabhi kisi teesri party ko nahi becha jata aur na hi ads ke liye use hota hai. Doston ko sirf wahi dikhta hai jo aap khud share karein (WhatsApp/Share message) — poora hisaab nahi.

Profile photo: aap ki photo koi bhi dekh sakta hai, lekin edit ya delete sirf aap kar sakte hain.

Account delete: account delete karwana ho to WhatsApp helpline par rabta karein — admin aap ka account aur data delete kar dega.
