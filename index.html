Privacy Policy — Easy Khata

Aap ka hisaab, aap ka data. Aap ke personal contacts private rehte hain. Community mein add kiye gaye transactions us community ke authorized members dekh sakte hain. Bina login ke hisaab sirf isi device par save hota hai; account ke sath aap apna hisaab doosre devices par bhi dekh sakte hain.

Kya save hota hai: aap ka naam, phone number, contacts, transactions, profile photo aur notifications ki settings.

Kya share nahi hota: aap ka data kabhi kisi teesri party ko nahi becha jata aur na hi ads ke liye use hota hai. Doston ko sirf wahi dikhta hai jo aap khud share karein (WhatsApp/Share message) — poora hisaab nahi.

Profile: aap ki profile ki maloomat community ke members ko dikh sakti hai. Aap apni profile details khud update kar sakte hain.

Account band karna: account band karwane ke liye WhatsApp helpline se rabta karein.
